What This Is

A ten-week residency for founders at the earliest stage, run by operators who have built and exited companies, not career program managers. You live in the house, you build every day, and you do it in public: the entire cohort is filmed for a docuseries distributed across our channels. That is distribution you cannot buy at your stage, and it starts the day you move in.

At the end of ten weeks you pitch at Demo Day in front of general partners from A-tier venture funds, the panel drawn from A-tier firms. Selected teams receive meaningful investment offers: the institutional funds at this table typically write seed checks of $500K to $3M, and the strongest teams leave Demo Day positioned to raise seed rounds of $1M to $5M.

The top three teams board a Gulfstream G5 from Seattle to San Francisco and pitch seven investors at 40,000 feet, one hour, fully televised.

The Program

Two core sessions every week, taught and pressure-tested by exited founders. And every week, on camera, the metrics hotseat: your numbers from last week, your target for next week, in front of the cohort. No hiding.

Wk 1
Foundation Week

Four sessions, the hardest week on purpose. Incorporation and equity done right: Delaware C-corp, splits, vesting, 83(b). Customer truth: real conversations, real signal, the wedge customer. Build discipline: scoping the two-week ship. Pricing and the first dollar.

Wk 2
Going to Market

Distribution and founder-led sales, then the enterprise motion: champions, procurement, when to chase logos.

Wk 3
The Growth Engine

Funnels, experiments, retention. Turning your first dollars into a repeatable motion.

Wk 4
Product Depth

Iterating off real usage data. The VC dinner circuit begins: dinners and meetings with Seattle-area funds, every week or two from here on.

Wk 5
Mid-Program Investor Review

Every team pitches a live panel for ratings and feedback. Halfway checkpoint, on camera.

Wk 6
Team & Operations

Hiring, burn, runway, financial hygiene. The unglamorous things that decide who survives.

Wk 7
Unit Economics & The Data Room

CAC, retention, margins, and the evidence pack an investor actually checks.

Wk 8
Fundraising Mechanics

SAFEs, caps, dilution math, what A-tier GPs look for at pre-seed.

Wk 9
Story & Pitch

Narrative, the deck, the two-minute version, camera-ready delivery.

Wk 10
Demo Day

Rehearsals, mock panels, final data pack. Then the real thing, and the jet.

Life in the House

Mornings start with yoga. Meals are chef-cooked at shared cost. The house car is yours to drive, and yes, it gets attention. Weekly builder events bring Seattle's founders, engineers, and hiring candidates through the door, VC dinners run from week 4, and weekends are for hikes out to the falls with the cohort. You are living with the seven other teams chasing the same thing, and that proximity is half the program.

SELECTION CRITERIA

How We
Score Founders.

One applicant, one page, roughly ten minutes. We work these steps in order and stop the moment a disqualifier fires.

Eligibility

All four must hold. Out of bounds is a referral, not a merit decline.

✓ Team of 1 to 4 (2 to 3 ideal)
✓ Pre-seed or earlier, no institutional round raised
✓ Full-time, in person, in Seattle for the entire cohort
✓ Willing to incorporate an investment-ready entity at the outset (or already has)

Disqualifiers

Any one of these is an immediate decline.

× Solo non-technical founder building a deeply technical product
× Cannot state clearly who the customer is
× Cannot state the business model
× Seeking a technical cofounder to build a vision they cannot build themselves
× Treating the venture as an interlude between jobs
× Story drifts or thins across successive conversations
× Projects obsession with no costly action behind it
× Corporate cadence or management overhead that conflicts with a chaotic early-cohort environment

Base Filter

At least one of these is required to advance.

A

Prior Founder

Exit, or a revenue-generating failure shut down for a defensible reason.

B

Prior Employment at Frontier Startup

Real tenure and ownership, not a logo. OpenAI, Anthropic, xAI, SpaceX, Tesla, Stripe, Palantir, Uber, PayPal, Airbnb, Coinbase, Scale AI, Perplexity, Cursor, Anduril, Sierra, Cognition, Hebbia.

C

Distribution + Capability

100k+ followers with a capability signal, or 1M+. Authenticity and engagement checked, not raw count.

D

Exceptional Young Builder

Shipped real usage, top contest finish, left a serious track to build, or an AI-native tool with traction.

Scoring Axes

Two independent axes, each scored 1 to 10.

Axis 1 — The Frontier Operator

9–10 Deep frontier domain expertise, a clear and defensible read of where the market is going, decisive in early conversation.
7–8 Real operator experience, some frontier exposure, vision still developing.
5–6 Operator depth without the frontier, or frontier exposure without operator depth.
3–4 Smart and capable but not at the frontier on anything specific.
1–2 Generic operator, no frontier position.

Axis 2 — Under the Spell

9–10 Undeniable. Cannot stop talking. Sacrifices already made. You do not know how you feel but you would write the check.
7–8 Clear obsession, real personal stakes, visibly controlled by the mission.
5–6 Genuine engagement but not yet obsessive, could fall either way.
3–4 Smart and engaged, no sign of the spell, doing it because they can.
1–2 Reads as a career move or a resume play.

Additional Signals

Demand Signal

Costly commitment present? A paid pilot, deposit, or design-partner engineering time. A non-binding LOI counts as near zero.

Thesis Alignment

Proprietary data flywheel present or accruing quickly. Regulated vertical with compliance as a barrier to entry. Network effects. Both data flywheel and regulation is the ideal profile.

Verdict Logic

ADMIT Base leg cleared, and Axis 1 of 7+ or an adjudicated Axis 2 of 7+.
PRIORITY High on both axes. Hell yes.
EXCEPTION Zero base leg with Axis 2 at 9 to 10. Flagged and routed, not declined.
HIGH BAR 5 to 6 on both with no compensating strength. Usually a pass unless market position makes losing nearly impossible.
DECLINE Any disqualifier fired.
